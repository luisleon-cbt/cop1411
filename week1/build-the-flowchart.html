How this challenge works

  1. Read the job in the round below. In building rounds, drag each symbol card onto an empty slot of the chart (or pick it in the slot's list). In other rounds, pick an answer or click the prompt parts.
  2. Press Run the chart (or Check). The token walks the chart; the feedback shows your points and why.
  3. Press Play the explanation to watch a working chart run with the real values.
  4. Press Next round. After round 10, review your results and press Try again if you want a better score.
