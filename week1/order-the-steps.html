How this challenge works

  1. Read the task in the round below. In rounds with scrambled steps, read every step first, then pick the order that works.
  2. Press Check. You see your points and why the best answer is best. Two swapped steps earn partial credit; a step that uses a value nobody has read yet earns nothing.
  3. Press Play the explanation to watch the board above fill the slots in order, or light the parts a prompt covers.
  4. Press Next round. After round 10, review your results and press Try again for a new shuffle.
