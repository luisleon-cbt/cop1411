How this challenge works

  1. Read the job in the round below. Drag the step cards into the order that works (or use the ▲ and ▼ buttons on each card). In a prompt round, drag the parts you need into the prompt box. In a question round, pick one answer.
  2. Press Run (or Check in a question round). The printer above prints the output, or jams and names the step that came too early. You see your points.
  3. Press Play the explanation to watch the working order in the scene.
  4. Press Next round. After the last round, review your results and press Try again if you want.
