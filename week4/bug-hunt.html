How this challenge works

  1. Read the situation and the symptom (the real output or error) in the round below. Read the Python in the box.
  2. On the flowchart above, click the symbol that causes the symptom, then press Check symbol (4 points; 2 on a second try).
  3. Fix the Python in the box and press Run. The tests run on real Python: 6 points when they all pass on your first Run, 4 on a later Run, 2 after Show the fix.
  4. Press Next round. After round 10, review your results and press Try again if you want.
