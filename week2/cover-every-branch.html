How this challenge works

  1. Read the round below. In a coverage round, click package cards on the shelf one at a time and watch the token light arrows green on the chart above. In a question round, pick one answer.
  2. Press Check coverage (or Check). You see your points and why: a dark arrow scores zero, and every extra package costs 2 points.
  3. Press Play the explanation to watch the best set of packages, or the right path, on the flowchart above.
  4. Press Next round. After round 10, review your results and press Try again if you want a better score.
