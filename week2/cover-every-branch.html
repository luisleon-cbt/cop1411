How this challenge works

  1. Read the round below. In a coverage round, click package cards one at a time: each one rides the floor above and lights the lines it runs in green. In the other rounds, pick one answer.
  2. Press Check (or Check coverage). You see your points and why the best answer is best.
  3. Press Play the explanation to watch the key package ride the floor again.
  4. Press Next round. After round 10, review your results and press Try again if you want a better score.
