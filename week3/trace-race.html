How this challenge works

  1. Read the round below. In a trace round, type a value in every cell of the table and in the "prints" box. In the other rounds, pick one answer.
  2. Press Run (trace rounds) or Check. The token walks the loop on the flowchart above and every cell flips green or red with the real value.
  3. Read the points and the explanation. Press Play the explanation to watch the token again.
  4. Press Next round. After round 10, review your results and press Try again if you want a better score.
