How this challenge works

  1. Read the code in the round below. Trace it on paper first: write one row per pass with the value of every variable. Then pick one answer.
  2. Press Check. You see your points and why the best answer is best.
  3. Press Play the explanation. The token goes around the loop on the board once per pass, and the trace table beside it fills row by row. Compare it with your paper.
  4. Press Next round. Rounds 9 and 10 show a Copilot explanation: first find the wrong sentence, then pick the trace table row that proves it wrong. After round 10, review your results and press Try again if you want.
